## Sound design cue sheet (NOT executed)

- A single click/tone when a probability gets selected. The previous tone cuts OFF when a new target overwrites it.
- Use one physically placed coin flick and tabletop landing per coin interaction; no generic slot-machine sounds while a character is moving.
- Rain/vehicles in the street have spatial progression: distant, approaching, passing; cave ambience becomes muffled after the fall.
- Cave should have audible breath and tiny rubble grit; first escape movement gets an escalating low rumble, not nonstop booms.
- Final truck engine grows just before the still frame. Cut sound abruptly to silence BEFORE impact; never depict impact.
- Music is optional; if used, find or create a properly licensed original bed. No commercial track presumed licensed.

## Recording and edit procedure

1. Record a clean original narrator track in any accessible editor or recorder (iPhone supported).
2. Align per-sentence starts and ends to recorded syllables, NOT the provisional scene windows.
3. Trim/extend shot durations to the actual voice rather than speeding up the narrator to force a predetermined length.
4. Link real audio cues to physically visible events, then check loudness and background sound balance.
5. Only after a separate user request to render, generate a motion review and final MP4. Current stage explicitly forbids rendering.
