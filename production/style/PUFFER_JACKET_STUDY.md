# Puffer jacket study

Owner requested a puffer jacket in black or another colour. This is an opt-in design comparison of matte/graphite black, cobalt blue and deep teal, with the same character height, pose and sunset light. A fourth panel enlarges the black collar and quilting; a separate still puts black in the existing lab.

Puffer construction adds a padded stand collar, gently scalloped torso outline, four rounded shaded baffles, recessed stitch channels, subtle upper-edge highlights, a central zip with one warm pull tab, slanted pockets and a gathered hem. Sleeves widen modestly and receive curved quilting lines. The neck stays visible, with a small warm lining/tee area. Light shoes separate the lower figure from the floor. Black uses graphite highlights instead of a featureless pure-black fill.

This extends existing outfit options and CharacterTurnV5 rather than replacing the rig. The previous hoodie, overshirt, utility jacket, waistcoat and cobalt lead jacket retain their default geometry/palettes. Existing movies and Probability Control do not adopt the puffer.

The padded shell adds about four local units per torso side and three units of sleeve diameter; the rig anchors and body height remain fixed. These are controlled costume-volume changes, not approval to make the underlying character bulky. The visible collar/hip/hand gaps must be checked in the rendered stills.

Design-only limitation: the sleeve stitching is currently defined for the resting pose and suppressed for posed arms. A chosen puffer must have its baffles and sleeve seams retargeted, overlap checked and contact/turn proofs rendered before animation adoption. The still study does not establish a finished animated costume.

Validation: TypeScript and actual rendered PNG review at 1440×1700 (study) and 1920×1080 (lab). Dedicated Actions workflow renders only those two PNGs, verifies their dimensions and uploads logs. No movie rerender is required to choose a colour.
