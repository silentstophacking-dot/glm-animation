# Cinematic character V2

Owner requested a comprehensive character/style revision after finding the earlier figure awkward compared with the supplied Ifverse videos. This is a new opt-in component; the previously accepted lab, detailed console, prior designs, motion proof and film remain available.

## Visual changes
Retains the small circular head, visible neck, elongated silhouette and balanced clothing volume, while replacing the rectangular garment with shaped shoulders, a tapered hoodie, curved hem/ribbing, wrapped hood, pocket seams and subtle directional fabric shading. Sleeves have rounded joints and a clearer fall. Trousers have tonal depth, and shoes now have a shaped toe, heel and sole rather than short line marks. The face supports calm, curious, confident, concerned and surprised states, with two-eye front/three-quarter placement and restrained brows/mouths. Head illumination stays a simple dark fill and warm rim; an initial gradient rendered an unwanted band in the contextual shot and was removed after inspecting the actual image.

## Prepared body mechanics
The old motion held a 15-unit hip drop through the walk. V2 computes required pelvis height from the leg reach/support geometry, reaching a maximum drop around 4.87 units on this sequence. Adds modest forward body lean and recoil, a far-arm counter-swing, and shoe rotation only inside the swing windows. Near-hand target is transformed into the leaning torso's local coordinates, so a body reaction does not detach the palm from the button. Head-facing changes alter the eye spacing, far-eye visibility and head width between front/three-quarter/profile-like states; this is not a complete rear-view turn library.

## Verification
All 270 planned frames pass reachable arm/leg checks and inherited foot-plant checks. The V2 palm contact audit reports zero reconstructed world-space error during the contact hold. Typecheck and actual rendered design/pose images are required; a passing audit does not prove natural movement. Full V2 gait playback and garment overlap review remain pending.

The design sheet compares the prior balanced drawing with relaxed and gesturing V2 figures and a face close-up. The four contextual poses reuse the original detailed lab/console and camera to make visual changes assessable. Both compositions are registered for manual selection and have isolated entries without remote font dependencies.
