# Character performance proof V3

The user's next priority is movement and acting. This 11-second silent proof preserves the current character design and lab, and leaves Probability Control untouched.

Acting order: eyes/head notice the console, body loads weight, three foot swings with planted support, pause and pullback, focused button press, light, recoil with raised free arm, release and relief. Head and torso turns have separate timing. Four locked camera setups keep the environmental planes and interaction anchors together.

The rig audit evaluates all 330 frames, enforces reachable limbs, checks foot drift outside swing windows, requires at least one supporting foot, and checks projected palm/button contact through press/release. These geometric checks support visual review; they do not prove natural acting or reference parity.

Manual render: select `PerformanceV3Proof` on the feature branch. Its single automatic production render includes the key-pose board, contact audit, H.264/yuv420p/BT.709 faststart output, complete decode/frame-count verification and diagnostics. Long-form composition selection remains available.
