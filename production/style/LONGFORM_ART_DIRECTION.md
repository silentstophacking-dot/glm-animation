## Object quality standard
A scene's important props must look designed and constructed, not like placeholder rectangles. At contact/reaction scale, show readable top/front/side planes, material-specific light response, seams or joins, supported structure and contact shadows. Use a small number of functional details that explain the object: a recessed control, a meter, a knob, a cable, a drawer pull, a shelf edge. Match warm window light and cool room fill across adjacent objects.

Glass uses transmitted colour and restrained reflection; painted metal uses broad tonal shading with a thin bevel highlight; shelves show depth and cast shade. Keep incidental details quieter and softer than the face and active hand/prop contact. Do not scatter noise, screws or glowing labels everywhere just to increase complexity. A wide can simplify distant detail while its hero interaction prop stays recognizable across inserts.
