# Clock insert V2 — material and depth correction

The owner liked the mini-scene but correctly identified that its clock insert abandoned the established visual standard: flat clock artwork on a nearly empty uniform wall. This revision addresses that specific shot and the clock's construction.

The cinematic clock uses an 18-unit extruded case with projected side facets, layered metal bevels, a recessed shaded dial, raised tapered hands and their moving shadows, a stepped center spindle, and a separate glass cover with restrained reflections. Warm upper-left highlights and cool case/fill tones follow the lab direction. Wall seams, conduit clamps, a mounting tab and a cast shadow give the object a physical attachment and room context. These are projected world surfaces; the background does not slide independently.

The insert uses a restrained truck/dolly to reveal case thickness. Clock rotation and audio timing are inherited from the same global story frame. The same detailed assembly appears in the new full-scene composition's wider shots. Reflections are authored planar marks, not ray-traced glass; the camera is a truck/dolly rather than a true orbit.
