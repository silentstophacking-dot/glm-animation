# Lab room depth proof

Owner requested a camera move through the existing lab to extend pseudo-3D into the background. This seven-second experiment uses the established sunset palette, skyline, glassware, control-unit artwork and black puffer character. It preserves earlier films and environment proofs rather than replacing Probability Control.

The room is reconstructed as layered surfaces and shallow solids through the existing projectWorld/planeTransform projection: rear architecture and window frame, a deeper city view, workbench cabinet and top slab, open bookcase with individual book/shelf depths, a four-legged interaction trolley with top/side surfaces, the enamel control unit and grounded foreground benches/glass. Faces are projected from world-space vertices, so top/side visibility changes with camera position. The new shot positions the sun on the left side of the window so its projected direction stays consistent with the character rim across the truck/dolly; previous compositions retain their original sun position. The original skyline and glassware are extracted as reusable art components; their earlier painted lab still uses the same shapes and colours.

One deterministic camera trucks right while dollying slightly forward and rising. The character stands still; its heel anchors, furniture feet and floor shadows use the shared world coordinates. Foreground glass bases sit on their bench tops, and the skyline is clipped through a separately projected window opening. No layer has an independently authored pan speed. Foreground travels more than interaction objects, which travel more than the wall and city. The move is not an orbit.

Local key-view review added visible supports under the foreground bench surfaces and retained the original varied lab glassware. The scenery remains stylized: cutout character and glassware, screen-space rim/blur/vignette, approximate floor shadows, no physical light simulation, no general-purpose hidden-surface renderer. The camera range and painter ordering are authored for this shot. Large orbits and passing through furniture need different occlusion/order handling.

Checks: the archived black-puffer lab still was re-rendered after extracting the artwork and compared pixel-for-pixel with the prior saved PNG; it is identical. TypeScript, 210-frame projection/support/parallax audit, rendered start/intermediate/end board, extracted movie frames, MP4 dimensions/fps/duration, decoded frame count, H.264/yuv420p/BT.709, faststart and complete decode. The geometry audit checks the actor's projected sole anchors against the floor and foreground glass against actual bench-top bounds/heights; it does not establish pixel-perfect contact or physical collision of every prop. Actual render review remains essential.

Compositions: LabRoomDepthProof and LabRoomDepthBoard. Automatic rendering is designated for this feature branch; manual composition selection and long-form production rendering are preserved. One full-resolution production movie, key-view PNG and diagnostic artifacts are generated. The existing acting proof and episode remain unchanged pending owner review of the camera/background result.
