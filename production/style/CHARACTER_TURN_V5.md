# Character turn V5

A six-second controlled front → three-quarter → profile turn using the custom Remotion 2.5D Character Rig skill. This is an isolated candidate built from the V4 garment, palette and proportion construction; it does not replace previous proofs or Probability Control.

The turn projects shoulder, elbow and wrist anchors separately, narrows an elliptical chest cross-section, moves the garment seam/pocket/drawstrings with the body, changes the visible side plane and retains a rear arm behind the torso. Head orientation leads the torso briefly. Visible eyes remain fully opaque; eye width and visibility follow the head surface. Head diameter and standing height stay constant.

Both heel anchors are fixed. The shoes pivot on the floor around those anchors, while hip projection changes and leg chains remain reachable. This is a constrained planted-heel test, not a general gait or a full locomotion rig. The front resting stance uses frontal arms and inward-facing thumbs; the shoe shapes also face front at the beginning.

References reviewed: supplied Reaction Time standing frame at 40s, plus the existing reference-comparison findings. These inform the slim profile, readable neck and simple face. No claim of source production methods, exact measurements or reference parity.

Local key-view inspection caught excessive knee bow in the first leg solve; raising the hip anchors slightly, without changing head height or garment, made the stance straighter. The final key-view sheet is shown before the motion proof. The short proof uses a fixed camera and neutral floor to make shape changes and foot contact easy to inspect.

Validation: TypeScript, 180-frame reachability/geometry/component audit, actual rendered key views and extracted movie frames, plus full MP4 metadata/frame-count/decode verification. Record concrete render results in the PR. Geometry checks do not establish natural motion or visual approval.

Known scope limits: right-facing quarter turn only, simplified hands, fixed light direction, no speech or contact with a prop, no validated acting/gait integration. Before replacing the performance character, retune contact targets and inspect intermediate garment/sleeve overlaps in that performance. The owner still reviews the result before episode-wide use.
